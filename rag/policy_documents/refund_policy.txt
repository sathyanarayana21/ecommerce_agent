REFUND POLICY

Full refunds are allowed for approved damaged-product returns.

Refunds should normally be processed using the original payment method.

A refund should only be processed after the return has been approved.

The refund amount for an approved damaged product is normally the original purchase price.

Refunds cannot be duplicated for the same order.

A refund that has already been initiated should not be initiated again.

The current customer support system uses simulated refunds. No real money is transferred.

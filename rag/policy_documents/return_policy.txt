RETURN POLICY

Customers can return delivered products within 7 days of the delivery date.

Damaged products are eligible for return verification.

The customer must provide the order ID and the reason for the return.

The return request must be verified before a refund is approved.

A full refund may be issued after successful return verification.

Products that are outside the 7-day return window are normally not eligible for return.

The return policy applies only to delivered products.
